# SHADOW OF XYRANTH — Deluxe Gold

> **The old computer rendered the pixels. Your imagination renders the fantasy world.**

**SHADOW OF XYRANTH — Deluxe Gold** is a standalone fantasy platform adventure from **Deluxe Gold Studios**, built around exploration, fair challenge, meaningful discovery, replayable mastery, and the spirit of classic computer games.

The player explores the kingdom of Xyranth, battles dangerous creatures, helps surviving characters, discovers relics and secrets, completes side objectives, and returns to previously visited regions to improve mastery and score.

---

## Why this project matters

SHADOW OF XYRANTH became the foundation of the **Alex + Astra** human–AI development collaboration and the wider Deluxe Gold Studios game-development program.

The project was developed through repeated cycles of:

- creative direction and gameplay design;
- implementation and code refinement;
- hands-on playtesting;
- defect reporting and targeted repair;
- regression testing;
- preservation of accepted baselines;
- UI, controls, accessibility, and responsive-display refinement;
- documentation and release preparation.

The goal was not simply to generate a game, but to **develop, test, refine, and stabilize a complete playable experience through sustained human–AI collaboration**.

## Deluxe Gold Studios philosophy

Deluxe Gold Studios games are built around a simple promise:

- **Curiosity matters.**
- **Mastery is earned.**
- **Challenge should be fair.**
- **Every interaction should feel meaningful.**
- **Presentation should support imagination.**

The studio respects the spirit of classic games without attempting to replace them. The aim is to create original handcrafted experiences that preserve exploration, timing, strategy, risk, reward, and discovery while benefiting from modern usability, accessibility, and player-focused polish.

> **Every game should reward curiosity, fair play, skill, imagination, and meaningful discovery.**

## Gameplay highlights

- Fantasy platform exploration across multiple regions
- Combat, hazards, enemies, relics, secrets, and side objectives
- NPC interactions and assistance goals
- Inventory and equipment systems
- Adventure Journal and Lore / NPC Codex
- Per-level progress and mastery tracking
- Medal and completion systems
- Save / load support
- High-score tracking
- Explorer mode for lower-pressure exploration
- Adjustable music and sound-effects volume
- High-contrast display option
- Optional hazard-impact camera shake
- Responsive interface designed for displays ranging from ordinary monitors to large high-resolution screens
- Keyboard, mouse, and gamepad-oriented play

All regions are available for exploration from the beginning. Reaching an exit clears a region, while full mastery rewards players for completing its broader checklist of objectives.

## AI-assisted development

SHADOW OF XYRANTH was created through a sustained human–AI development workflow.

The human side of the collaboration provided creative direction, game design, real gameplay evaluation, acceptance and rejection decisions, and long-term quality control. The AI side assisted with implementation, systems work, debugging, technical analysis, documentation, and iterative refinement.

The finished game is a **standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript experience**. It does **not require an AI model or OpenAI API at runtime**; AI was part of the development process rather than a dependency of the player's experience.

## Running the game
